1. What happened?
    EY, one of the Big Four accounting firms experienced a major data breach in March 2026.
    In July, a few months after the incident, the company warned clients that their sensitive financial and personal information was compromised.
2. Who was impacted?
    The impacted companies include investment bank Goldman Sachs, investment fund Man Group, and real estate developer Tishman Speyer. Personal information and financial information was accessed including names, addresses, social security numbers, account numbers, credit/debit card numbers, and other types of information. 
3. What was the cause?
    Ey has not shared details on the cause of the attack or who did it. Nobody has claimed responsibility for the incident as of now.
