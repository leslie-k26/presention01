# EY Data Breach 2026
1. What happened?
    Ernst & Young (EY) experienced a data breach between March 28 and April 12, 2026 where unauthorized actors downloaded documents containing sensitive personal and financial information of clients. In July, a few months after the incident, the company warned clients that their sensitive financial and personal information was compromised. EY is one of the world's largest accounting firms, handling tax operations for many companies. 
2. Who was impacted?
    Among the impacted companies are investment bank Goldman Sachs, investment fund Man Group, and real estate developer Tishman Speyer. Personal information and financial information was accessed including names, addresses, social security numbers, account numbers, credit/debit card numbers, and other types of information. 
3. What was the cause?
    The Shinyhunters extortion group claimed responsibility and threatened to leak the data. The attackers intruded into a third-party support ticket platform to bypass security measures. They exploited the trusted relationship between EY and its vendor. These stolen credentials allegedly allowed them to breach Ernst & Young's Jira, GitHub, and Azure environments.

4. Lessons to learn:
    Companies not only need to be responsible of their own security but also the security of their trusted vendors. Organizations need to review the security of their trusted vendors and third party services.
